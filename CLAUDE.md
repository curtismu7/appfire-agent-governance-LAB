# appfire-agent-governance-LAB

Demo-tier standalone app (catalog D24): the customer-facing AI agent of AI-DEMO2 with all 87 use cases in eight tracks. Engine plus Real; an owner-only identity-provider-only path reaches the identity provider only. **Step 3 of 3 (integration)**: Live mode, doctor/health reporting of it, hub and talk-track entries.
